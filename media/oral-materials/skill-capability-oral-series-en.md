# Codex Video Workflow Promo Narration

## Positioning

- Video type: spoken product explainer
- Audience: creators, engineers, and operators who want Codex to produce a reviewable local video package
- Core promise: this skill does more than export an MP4. It turns a brief into a planned, narrated, animated, covered, QC-checked, evidence-backed video package.
- Rights boundary: original English narration; local generated visuals; local system TTS for this English promo; no voice cloning; no stock media; no external platform assets.

## Narration

When people ask Codex to make a video, the risky part is rarely the final export. The risky part is everything that gets skipped before export. Codex Video Workflow treats the job as a production system, not a one-off render. First, it turns the brief into a contract: platform, aspect ratio, rights boundaries, voice style, subtitle policy, cover targets, timing strategy, and QC gates. Then it writes the spoken material before TTS. You get the source script, the spoken script, frame-bound narration segments, and voice direction, so pauses stay after complete ideas instead of breaking sentences. The visuals are planned before rendering too. The workflow selects a presentation system, an aesthetic brief, a motion template, and a visual metaphor for every scene, then records image prompts and local fallback assets. For voice, it stays local by default and records the backend, loudness processing, silence checks, and subtitle timing. At the end, the package is not just a video file. It includes covers, platform cover variants, screenshots, ffprobe output, black-frame checks, volume logs, a quality scorecard, and a local delivery page. That is the point of this skill: content logic first, narration and motion second, and evidence at the end so the video is watchable, audible, and reviewable.

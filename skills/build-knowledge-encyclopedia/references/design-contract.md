# Knowledge Encyclopedia Design Contract

## Content schema

- One subject only.
- One identity statement and three to five supported facts.
- One mechanism, habit, or cause-effect explanation.
- One visualized rating or comparison with an explicit scale.
- Detail callouts must point to real subject features.

## Visual language

Use a modern editorial encyclopedia plate: warm off-white paper, precise soft naturalist illustration, modular panels, thin two-tone icons, muted sage accent, subtle shadows, consistent corner radii. Establish a strong title, hero subject, two useful callouts, a fact/mechanism panel, and a compact score strip. Keep the bottom safe band quiet.

## Reject when

- The page could be mistaken for a specimen collection card.
- Fact panels contain meaningless strokes instead of teachable information.
- Rating icons and numeric score disagree.
- Discrete rating markers are not explicitly countable. For a 9/10 rating, render exactly ten markers in a visible 5+5 grouping: five filled, then four filled and one outline.
- A detail inset shows a feature not belonging to the subject.
- A callout line does not terminate on the exact feature named by its label.
- The subject illustration overwhelms the explanation or enters the subtitle band.

## Repair map

- Weak teaching value: replace generic modules with one mechanism diagram and one evidence-backed comparison.
- Atlas-card confusion: emphasize cross-attribute synthesis and rating; remove collection numbering and specimen-card framing.
- Crowding: reduce to one hero, two callouts, and three modules before shrinking type.
- Unsafe score strip: move the entire information system above the 82% boundary; a quiet-looking card still fails if any of its border or icons cross that line.
- Count drift: regenerate or edit only the rating strip, then recount every marker; do not accept an approximate icon run.

## Forward-test brief

Create a 9:16 vertical page titled “章鱼的三颗心脏”. Explain that two branchial hearts pump blood to the gills and one systemic heart pumps oxygenated blood to the body. Show a precise octopus hero, a simplified three-heart mechanism diagram, three supported facts, and a “伪装能力 9/10” score. Use exact Chinese only for the title, the three heart labels, and the score. Keep the lower 18% empty.

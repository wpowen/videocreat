# Strategy Guide Design Contract

## Content schema

- A concrete outcome and any prerequisite.
- Three to seven ordered steps, each beginning with an action.
- A checklist of observable completion conditions.
- One to three specific pitfalls paired with corrections.
- A visible final success signal.

## Visual language

Use a restrained premium flat-vector guide board: light neutral ground, strict modular grid, electric blue numbers and route line, red restricted to pitfall modules, crisp icons, generous card spacing, and a clear top-to-bottom reading path. Design for action, not decoration.

## Reject when

- Step numbers skip, repeat, or the path flows backward.
- Checklist rows are empty placeholder lines.
- Red appears outside warnings or warnings lack a corrective action.
- More than one main lesson competes on the page.
- Any card, route, or icon occupies the lower 18% safe band.
- Any unwhitelisted English UI heading or generic success label appears merely to decorate the board.
- A pictorial gauge, comparison, or diagram invents extra explanatory labels outside the exact text plan; use icon-only states when those words are not required.

## Repair map

- Generic board: replace icons with action-specific diagrams and real checklist items.
- Dense page: split into overview plus stage pages rather than shrinking everything.
- Unsafe final stage: shorten illustrations or move checklist/pitfall content earlier; do not solve the problem by leaving only a cosmetic bottom margin.
- Weak hierarchy: enlarge step markers and reduce decorative UI chrome.
- Extra labels: remove them without weakening the action diagram; preserve the exact step, checklist, and pitfall copy.

## Forward-test brief

Create a 9:16 board titled “手机拍采访的 4 步布光法”. Steps: choose a window as key light; place the subject 45 degrees to the window; use a white foam board as fill; lock exposure on the face. Checklist: eyes have catchlight, background is darker than face, skin is not overexposed. Pitfall: mixing window daylight with a warm ceiling lamp; correction: turn the ceiling lamp off. Keep the lower 18% empty.

# Surreal Carrier Design Contract

## Content schema

- One topic and one physical carrier.
- A one-to-one mapping from visible elements to the topic.
- One directional process or transformation.
- A defined hook, transition, or echo function.
- A planned overlay zone with low visual complexity.

## Visual language

Use cinematic photorealistic surrealism with one sculptural carrier, controlled material realism, elegant restraint, and a single warm or cool key color. Internal worlds must feel physically integrated into the carrier. Surroundings stay minimal and do not become generic fantasy scenery.

## Reject when

- The image is impressive but the metaphor cannot be explained in one sentence.
- More than one carrier competes for attention.
- The process direction is visually ambiguous.
- Generic landscapes or particles replace topic-specific meaning.
- No calm overlay space remains or the carrier fills the subtitle band.
- Paper, screens, diagrams, or fragments contain readable or pseudo-readable marks when the text policy is `no-text`.

## Repair map

- Generic: replace scenery with topic-specific objects and remove unexplained props.
- Unclear process: add a single visual flow through the carrier and remove secondary motion.
- Crowded: reduce carrier scale and reserve one clean negative-space column.
- Pseudo text: replace marked paper with blank geometric fragments or non-linguistic nodes and connectors.

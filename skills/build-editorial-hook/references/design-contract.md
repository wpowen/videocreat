# Editorial Hook Design Contract

## Content schema

- One hook role and one viewer promise.
- One dominant subject with a visible conceptual tension.
- A headline no longer than 14 Chinese characters when possible.
- Optional kicker shorter than the headline.
- A clean handoff into the next narration beat.

## Visual language

Use premium editorial art direction: generous margins, print-quality typography zone, controlled light, tactile material, and one restrained signal color. The subject may be photographed, illustrated, or sculptural, but a run uses one medium consistently. The title and subject form one deliberate geometry.

## Reject when

- The frame reads as a product ad rather than an editorial idea.
- Copy is unreadable at thumbnail size or repeats the same meaning twice.
- Subject, face, or device enters the subtitle-safe band.
- Decorative issue lines, stickers, barcode, or fake masthead appear.
- Composition repeats the same black-stage/red-line formula without topic justification.

## Repair map

- Ad-like: remove sales polish, brand-like device framing, and unnecessary product glamour; strengthen the editorial metaphor.
- Weak hook: shorten copy and make the visible tension concrete.
- Repetition: switch composition family while retaining margins, hierarchy, and signal-color discipline.

## Forward-test brief

Create a 9:16 opening question frame titled “你不是没时间” with kicker “是注意力被切碎了”. Dominant subject: a translucent daily calendar physically fragmented into many small glowing notification-shaped shards, with one intact two-hour block at the center. Warm ivory studio stage, charcoal type zone, one cobalt signal accent. No device, app UI, logo, fake magazine details, or extra text. Keep the lower 18% calm.
